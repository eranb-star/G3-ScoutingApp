## Match-practice refinement — 28 September
- Correct approved motto: **Glued by Passion** (supersedes “with”).
- Investigated the apparent 19-second autonomous limit: EngineeringPlanner.tsx defaults reserveSeconds to 1 (including missing values on workspace load); autonomousPlanning.ts treats insufficient reserve as an error separately from exceeding official duration. Thus a 20-second season with default reserve accepts only 19 seconds. Existing workaround: Time reserve and partner areas → Time to keep in reserve = 0. Required UX: display official duration and optional reserve separately; full 20-second preview must be possible, with reserve shortfall a clearly distinguished planning warning rather than an unexplained game-rule failure. Keep an actual duration overflow distinct; verify boundary/fractional times and saved plans.
- Add full-match practice alongside existing 60-second drills (twinPractice.ts currently fixes PRACTICE_SECONDS=60). Official 2026 timing: 20 s AUTO + 140 s TELEOP; TELEOP has 10 s transition, four 25 s shifts and 30 s endgame. Source: https://www.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/volunteer/frc/2026-rebuilt-cheat-sheet.pdf and official manual https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/Manual/HTML/2026GameManual.htm . Recheck latest rule updates before release, including AUTO-to-TELEOP delay and score-credit grace windows; do not silently conflate those with the scoring clock.
- Full-match flow: choose robot/alliance/preload and autonomous plan → ready/countdown → autonomous playback → driver handoff → timed shifts/endgame → automatic stop/results/replay. If no autonomous plan, explicitly offer teleop-only or labeled manually driven rehearsal, not a false autonomous execution. Pre-start countdown does not consume official period time.
- Hub visual activation and scored-fuel eligibility must share the authoritative match-state clock; both active in AUTO, transition and endgame, alternate during shifts according to official AUTO fuel result. Implement official tie selection and provide a visible opponent-result setup for solo practice; do not infer full alliance result from one robot alone. Verify exact lights/countdown transition signals against current manual. Preserve practice vs official scoring labels until all applicable scoring supported.
- Competition audio requested: start/period/end/countdown cues, mute/volume, browser audio unlocking from Start, no overlap or replay duplication; verify official audio availability/reuse permission before distributing exact recordings, otherwise clearly label synthetic practice cues. Sound, HUD, hub lights and score gating use the same state across web/mobile/VR. Real headset/device acceptance remains required.
