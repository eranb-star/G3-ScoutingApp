## Account ownership decision — do this first

Prefer the school or supporting registered organization as publisher if it is willing and able to authorize enrollment. A robotics team name alone does not prove legal entity status. Use actual legal name/address and an authorized adult representative, not an invented organization or a student's personal ownership. Establish recovery contacts and delegated access rather than sharing passwords.

If no appropriate organization can enroll, an adult individual account is an alternative, with personal publisher identity and applicable Google testing requirements. Do not choose organization merely to avoid testing. Apple individual seller identity is the individual's legal name; organization enrollment requires authority and generally a D-U-N-S record. Organization Google accounts also require legal verification and D-U-N-S information, subject to Google's documented exceptions. Confirm school eligibility before enrollment.

Owner prepares: legal owner/name/address, accessible email/phone, website, authority to act for the organization, D-U-N-S if required, identity/organization evidence, payment method, recovery contacts and two-factor authentication. Do not put identity documents, secrets, signing keystores or account passwords into Git or chat.

Costs: Google Play Console currently US$25 once; Apple Developer Program generally US$99/year or local pricing. Eligible accredited educational/nonprofit organizations may qualify for Apple's fee waiver; eligibility is not assumed. Mac/build-service costs are separate and no new paid service is authorized by this guide.

## Android — owner and engineering steps

1. **Owner: create the developer account.** Sign in at [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/) with the chosen owner account. Select the correct account type, complete identity/organization/contact verification, accept terms and pay the registration fee. Complete any device/identity verification the console requires. Do not create duplicate app entries if one is later found under another owner.
2. **Owner: create the app record.** Choose Create app, G3's display name, default language, App, and Free if this remains the intended model. Provide support contact information. Add Hebrew/English store copy as appropriate. This does not grant anyone access to G3's private content.
3. **Engineering + owner: settle signing BEFORE distribution.** We already have sideloaded installations. In Play App Signing, use the supported flow to retain/provide the existing app-signing key and verify the resulting signing certificate. Do not blindly accept a new unrelated key. Follow Play Console's encrypted key-transfer procedure; never send the raw key in chat/email. Separate upload-key management from the app-signing identity. If retention cannot be configured, stop and explicitly plan a data-safe migration rather than asking students to uninstall. Test the Play-delivered update over 2.6.1 before wider rollout. Register actual Play signing fingerprints with any provider that requires them; upload-key fingerprints alone are insufficient.
4. **Engineering: generate a current AAB.** Use the same reduced-asset Android build, Capacitor copy and signed Gradle `bundleRelease`, with a versionCode above the installed/published maximum (31 is the next local candidate, subject to Console). The Android build must retain the 2026 field/KitBot and small components and route optional models online. Standard web production build must not be trimmed. Existing Java21 toolchain applies. Verify the bundle, actual generated APK signing, size, permissions, target API and required 16KB page-size support for native libraries. Current APK targets API36; upload-time requirements still need checking. Do not rename `.apk` to `.aab`.
5. **Engineering + owner: prepare store readiness.** Publish a real public privacy policy and support contact/page; prepare any required account/data-deletion path based on how accounts are actually created and managed. Audit collection/sharing rather than assuming a login-only team app is exempt. Cover attendance/location checks, member information, uploaded content, push tokens, diagnostics, and optional AI/CAD/code providers. Disclose actual retention and authorized review/data use; do not automatically erase auditable finance/attendance records. Inspect age audience, applicable minors requirements, content rating, ads declaration, data safety and permissions. No complete store-readiness audit is claimed yet.
6. **Owner: fill store listing and App content.** Upload icon, feature graphic and screenshots at current Console-required sizes, descriptions, support/privacy URLs; complete rating, audience, ads, app-access and Data safety questionnaires accurately. Internal-only testing may have different form requirements, but production/closed readiness still needs these checks. Do not classify the app as financial services just because it records internal expenses; answer based on actual functions.
7. **Engineering: provide reviewer access safely.** Use an isolated synthetic review account/environment that demonstrates required features without real student/finance/private CAD exposure. Reviewers may be away from the workshop; explain or safely demonstrate location-restricted attendance without weakening real production checks. Provide working credentials/instructions through the store's private app-access fields. Review existing channels/media moderation/reporting before claiming user-generated-content compliance. Screenshots must not expose real student details.
8. **Owner: start Internal testing.** Under Test and release → Testing → Internal testing, select a tester email list/Google Group as supported, create a release and upload the AAB with release notes. Review warnings and publish the test. Add a few mentors/leaders first. Internal testing supports up to 100 testers; it is a practical first team-install route, not public discoverability.
9. **Students/mentors: install.** Open the invitation/opt-in link while signed into the same Google account added as a tester; join, follow the Play Store link and install/update. Use the ordinary G3 login. Developer-account membership is not required for testers. Validate an upgrade from the sideloaded APK, actual push receipt, login/files/attendance, current field offline and optional assets online. Do not instruct everyone to uninstall on an error; investigate certificate/version/account mismatch first.
10. **Owner + testers: closed test when needed.** New personal accounts created after 13 November 2023 currently need at least 12 testers opted into the closed test continuously for 14 days before applying for production access. Keep meaningful feedback and address defects. Internal testing is not a substitute for that closed-test requirement. A tester in the internal track must opt out of internal and opt into closed for that route. Organization accounts follow their own Console requirements; do not assume all verification is waived.
11. **Owner: choose distribution endpoint.** For the pilot use internal/closed testing. For a stable general Play listing, complete eligibility/review then publish production to chosen countries. The app can remain login/role restricted even if the listing is public. Managed Google Play private distribution is a different organizational/device-management option and should only be chosen if the school already uses it. Do not assume regular team phones have that setup.
12. **Ongoing engineering/owner:** increase versionCode, build/upload tested AAB, review pre-launch/crash reports, then roll out. Play manages updates subject to user settings. Website deployment still does not replace the bundled native UI. Keep the old version/server API compatible during rollout; a rollback normally needs a new higher-version build.

## iPhone — immediate access, then native beta, then stable distribution

### Use the existing website now

1. On iPhone open Safari and https://g3-6740.com.
2. Open Share, select Add to Home Screen (add it to Share actions if necessary), enable Open as Web App if that option is shown, then Add.
3. Open the new icon and log in with the normal G3 account.

This is website access, not an iOS native build. Do not promise the Android native push/Wi-Fi functionality or bundled offline field assets here. Existing browser check-in capabilities must be tested on the actual iPhone at the workshop. [Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-lamr/guide/iphone/iphea86e5236/ios).

### Native iPhone preparation and TestFlight

1. **Owner: enroll.** Use an Apple Account with two-factor authentication; enroll the authorized school/organization or adult individual in the standard [Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/). Complete verification and pay or obtain an eligible fee waiver. The Enterprise Program is not the recommended route for this student team.
2. **Owner: arrange a Mac/build path.** Native iOS compilation/signing needs Apple's toolchain on macOS (local Mac or separately approved hosted service). Windows alone cannot archive this iOS app. Use a currently App Store-supported Xcode/SDK; no specific future version is assumed. Arrange a real iPhone too. [Capacitor setup](https://capacitorjs.com/docs/getting-started/environment-setup), [Apple upcoming requirements](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/).
3. **Engineering: create the iOS target.** Install the matching Capacitor iOS package, add/sync iOS, set the chosen available Bundle ID, icons, supported devices/orientations and signing team. Reuse the web application and backend; do not rewrite every feature in Swift. Generalize the Android-only reduced-asset build policy for iOS rather than accidentally bundling all models again.
4. **Engineering: implement actual platform support.** Add APNs/native push (or a correctly configured FCM-to-APNs path) and backend platform/token handling, logout/revocation and foreground/background behavior. Review WifiInfo usage: iOS does not offer the same general Wi-Fi access as Android. Use permitted capabilities or the existing valid browser/location route, retaining server-side presence rules and clear permission errors. Test OAuth/browser return, login persistence, files/share/download, photo/library usage, safe areas, keyboard, Hebrew/RTL, 3D memory/context recovery and asset cache behavior. Include required purpose strings/privacy manifests for the actual code/SDKs; telemetry must match privacy declarations.
5. **Owner + engineering: app record and compliance.** Register the app identity and create an iOS app record in App Store Connect; set name/language/Bundle ID/SKU. Configure app privacy, support/privacy URLs, age rating, export-compliance responses based on actual encryption, screenshots and reviewer information. Account-deletion obligations depend on actual account-creation flows; inspect and implement applicable requirements before claiming readiness. Review safe handling/moderation of team-uploaded content and AI disclosure. A useful native experience and review acceptance must be demonstrated, not assumed because a web wrapper compiles.
6. **Engineering: archive/upload.** In Xcode select the correct team/release target, archive for a generic iOS device, validate and distribute to App Store Connect. Wait for processing; address errors and export-compliance questions. The artifact is an iOS build, never the Android APK. [Upload builds](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-builds/upload-builds/).
7. **Owner: invite external TestFlight testers.** In App Store Connect → app → TestFlight, create an external testing group, fill beta description/contact/reviewer login information, select the build and submit it for Beta App Review where required. After approval, invite students by email; use a controlled invitation group rather than automatically exposing a public beta link. Internal testers are App Store Connect users—students do not need developer-console roles just to test. [External testing](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/invite-external-testers/).
8. **Students: install Apple's TestFlight.** Download the official TestFlight app from the App Store, open the invitation on the iPhone, accept it, select Install, open G3 and use the existing G3 login. Students do not buy developer memberships. Report device/iOS/build and reproducible issues. TestFlight builds expire after 90 days, so beta is not an unattended permanent distribution solution. [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/), [overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/testflight-overview/).
9. **Team + engineering: acceptance.** Test installation/update, session restoration, real absence notifications in foreground/background, permitted check-in and permission denial, files, key student/leader screens, current field offline and first optional model load. Most testing can happen at home; actual attendance-presence tests require workshop access. Fix concrete failures before widening the beta.

### Stable iPhone distribution

10. **Owner + engineering: submit App Store release.** Complete app information, listing/privacy/age-rating forms, screenshots, build selection and private review credentials/notes. Submit for App Review, answer review questions and release when accepted. TestFlight approval is not App Store approval. [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [App privacy](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy/).
11. **Recommended team option: request Unlisted App distribution after preparing a review-ready release.** Apple approval is required. Unlisted means installable through a direct App Store link, absent from ordinary discovery; anyone with the link may reach it, so G3 authentication and authorization remain essential. It is not a private-data access control. Alternatively choose a normal public listing with restricted G3 login. Apple School Manager custom distribution is a separate option only if the school manages devices/accounts that way. [Unlisted distribution](https://developer.apple.com/support/unlisted-app-distribution/).
12. **Ongoing:** maintain membership, signing/profiles/APNs credentials, supported SDKs, store privacy accuracy and timely releases. Users get normal App Store updates after stable distribution; TestFlight updates continue only for beta participants. Recheck real notifications after certificate/provider/platform changes.
