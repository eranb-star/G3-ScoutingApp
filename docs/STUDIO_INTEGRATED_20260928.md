Ties select and retain a random order for the run. Inactive hubs process/return balls but do not award fuel credit outside the rule grace window. Hub status rings show activity, deactivation pulse and transition white flash; these are training visual indicators, not CAD-certified LED hardware reproductions. Exact official recordings were not found with confirmed redistribution terms; do not claim identical competition audio. Timing/state derives from the same match engine. VR uses the same engine, retains controller deadman/visibility/tracking gates, and now places the observer at the selected red/blue driver station.
